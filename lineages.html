Lineages

Guru to disciple, down to the present day — and where the lines touch.

Tree Guide

A paramparā (परम्परा, "one after another") is a chain of teachers, each passing on what they received . Six branches are traced here to teachers alive today. Tap anyone in the tree to see who taught them and whom they taught.

Advaita root Gauḍapāda → Govinda → Śaṅkara
Ramakrishna Vivekananda → Sarvapriyananda
Kriyā Yoga Babaji → Yogananda → SRF & Ananda
Chinmaya Sivananda → Chinmayananda
Ramana Arunachala → Muruganār · Papaji · Lakṣmaṇa
Patañjali Yoga Sūtras → Kriyā · Rāja Yoga · Haṭha
Nāth · Inchegiri Gorakhnāth → Nisargadatta

Between the lineages

The branches share a root but never merged. They cross in a few documented meetings and run in parallel at several points.
