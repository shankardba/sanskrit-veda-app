Lineages

Guru to disciple, down to the present day — and where the lines touch.

A paramparā (परम्परा, "one after another") is a chain of teachers, each passing on what they received. Two such chains are charted here: the Advaita line of Ramakrishna → Vivekananda, carried to the present by the Ramakrishna Order and Swami Sarvapriyananda, and the Kriyā Yoga line of Babaji, Lahiri Mahasaya, Sri Yukteswar and Paramahansa Yogananda. Lahiri Mahasaya is the link usually skipped between Babaji and Yukteswar. Both lines take their monastic vows from the orders traditionally traced to Ādi Śaṅkara. Select anyone to see who they learned from and who they taught.

Between the lineages

The two lines never merged, but they cross at one person and run in parallel at several points.
