Mathematics / Vedic Mathematics

Vedic Mathematics — all 40 chapters, 71 lessons

Each sūtra is a one-line Sanskrit aphorism naming a method — Ūrdhva-Tiryagbhyām, “vertically and crosswise”; Nikhilaṁ Navataścaramaṁ Daśataḥ, “all from nine and the last from ten”. Pick a lesson — in the book’s chapter order or grouped by sūtra — watch the digits move, try your own numbers, then practise. Tīrtha attributed the sūtras to an appendix of the Atharvaveda; they are not found in the known texts, and historians regard the system as his own — the panel beside each lesson shows the algebra that makes it work either way.
